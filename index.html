Route Chat wireframe

One input for every Route product. Ask it to do something and it hands you the right Route widget, prefilled. Ask it something and it gives you a short, sourced rundown. Nothing moves until you sign.

  • 1Chat is a tab, not a takeover. Sits first in the existing nav. Swap and the rest stay one click away.

Open questions for Routeanswers change the design

The agent

  1. Does the agent ever act without a signature (agent wallet, session keys, DCA, auto-trading), or is it always "prepare, you sign"?must ask
  2. Which actions are in v1: swap, limit, multi-swap, Earn deposit/withdraw, LP, borrow? Anything outside Route like transfers or bridging?must ask
  3. Who builds the agent and its intent parsing? Are we designing only the UI, or also response formats and copy?
  4. Which chains: Robinhood Chain only, or Arc / Solana / Base like the screener?

Research

  1. What data does it pull from: onchain, Route's own screener/analytics, X, the open web? Can it cite sources?must ask
  2. Policy on "who's behind X": is naming people OK, and what does it say when a team is anon?
  3. Tokenized stocks (SPY, NVDA, TSLA): any legal limits on research or anything that reads as advice?
  4. Fixed rundown template for tokens, or free-form answers?

Product + brand

  1. Where does it live: tab, home page, ⌘K overlay, or a separate app? Is mobile the priority?
  2. Wallet required to chat? Is history saved, and per wallet?
  3. Any gating: ROUTE holders only, usage limits, paid tier? (ties into "Vault Utility" on the roadmap)
  4. Does the agent have a name and personality, or is it just "Route"? Tone: degen or neutral?
  5. Light, dark or both? Deadline, and what fidelity do they need next?
